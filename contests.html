How judging works

  1. Clarity (30%)

    Can a first-time visitor tell what you sell and why it's for them within five seconds?

  2. Product pages (25%)

    Photos, descriptions, pricing and trust signals.

  3. Improvement (25%)

    How much better it is than the "before", or how complete a brand-new launch is.

  4. Originality (20%)

    A distinct brand, voice and offer.

No purchase necessary. Entering is free, and buying any product or service does not improve your chances of winning. The challenge is void where prohibited or restricted by law.

  • Organiser: GetYourStore.com.
  • Eligibility: individuals aged 18+ (or the age of majority in their jurisdiction) who own or manage the store entered. Organiser staff and their immediate families are not eligible. Residents of jurisdictions where such contests are prohibited may not enter.
  • Period: each challenge runs from the 1st day of the calendar month until 23:59 (entrant's local time) on its last day. One entry per person per month.

  • Notification: winners are notified by email within 14 days of the close and must reply within 14 days, or an alternate winner may be chosen.
  • Content: entrants keep ownership of their store and content, and grant the organiser a licence to feature the store name, URL, screenshots and description for promoting the challenge.
  • Conduct: entries promoting illegal, misleading, adult or infringing products are disqualified.
  • Privacy: entry data is used only to run the challenge, as described in our Privacy Policy.
  • General: the organiser may modify or cancel a challenge if circumstances beyond its control prevent it from running fairly. Decisions of the judges are final.
