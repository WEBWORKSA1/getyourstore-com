How to choose in five minutes

  1. Start from what you sell. Digital-only? A creator platform will be faster than any full store builder. Selling in person? Prioritise point of sale. Handmade? A marketplace can test demand cheaply.
  2. Be honest about maintenance. Open-source gives you control, but someone has to update it. Hosted platforms handle security and uptime for you.
  3. Model the fees at your expected volume with the fee calculator, not at today's zero.
  4. Check your must-haves: subscriptions, B2B pricing, product options, multiple currencies, local payment methods.
  5. Confirm you can export products, customers and orders, so you're never locked in.
