## Patterns that matter (and how GetYourStore.com uses them)

1. **Free tools are the traffic engine.** Sellfy, Salehoo, Spocket, Printify, ecommerce-platforms.com and Shopify all use calculators and generators to pull search traffic. → 8 free tools, each on its own SEO page.
2. **Quiz → recommendation → CTA.** websitebuilderexpert.com and ecommerce-platforms.com both run platform quizzes. → Platform Picker quiz feeding the lead form.
3. **Done-for-you setup is the money.** Storetasker and ecommerce-platforms.com make money from setup services. → "Get Your Store Built" multi-step brief plus a free store-audit form.
4. **Long-form step guides with jump links and video** (WPBeginner, Wix). → 10-step roadmap + 12 original guides + a video library.
5. **Trust without fake proof.** Methodology pages, editorial policy, fee transparency. → Editorial policy, "how we rate" notes, no invented testimonials.
6. **Supporter patterns** (Buy Me a Coffee): preset amounts, monthly tiers, a choice of what the money funds. → Support page with allocation picker.
7. **Short, human-reviewed applications** (eCommerceFuel). → Careers / expert-network application and contest entry.

## Concept decision

**GetYourStore.com = the free "launch your online store" hub + done-for-you store build lead engine.**

Why this beats the alternatives (marketplace, store builder, directory):
- **The domain is the call to action.** "Get your store" matches high-intent queries (start an online store, best ecommerce platform, Shopify vs WooCommerce, store name ideas).
- **High-value advertisers.** Ecommerce platforms, payments, hosting and business-formation advertisers bid on this audience, so AdSense pays more per page here than in general content niches.
- **Leads are worth far more than ad views.** A store build is a $1,500–$10,000 project. One closed lead a month outearns thousands of ad pageviews, and leads can be fulfilled through the expert network (careers page) or sold to agencies.
- **Zero inventory and zero platform risk.** It's static, free to host on GitHub Pages, and every tool runs in the browser.

Revenue stack (in order of expected value): store-build leads → AdSense (Auto ads) → sponsorships/featured placements → supporter contributions → future affiliate links (disclosed).
