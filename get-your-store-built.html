How much does a store build cost?

It depends on product count, design and features. Simple launches sit at the lower end of the budget ranges above; custom design, large catalogues and migrations cost more. You get one fixed price before any work starts.

Which platform will you use?

Whichever fits your products, budget and growth plans. We work across the major hosted and open-source platforms and explain the reasoning in your plan.

Do I own the store?

Yes. Accounts, domain, content and data are set up in your name. You can take it anywhere.

How long does it take?

Most launches take 2–4 weeks from approval, depending on product count and how quickly content arrives. Rush options are available.

What if I just want advice?

Send the brief anyway and mention it in the notes. We can do a one-off strategy session instead of a build.
