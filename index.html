FAQ

Quick answers

How much does it cost to start an online store?

A lean store can launch for a few hundred dollars in the first year: a platform plan, a domain and a small marketing budget. Inventory, apps, design and paid ads raise that. Model your own numbers with the Startup Cost Estimator.

Which ecommerce platform is best for beginners?

It depends on what you sell and how much control you want. Hosted platforms are easiest to start; open-source options give more control but need more upkeep. The 60-second quiz gives you a personalised shortlist.
