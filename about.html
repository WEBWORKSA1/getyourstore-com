About us

Helping more people get their store live

GetYourStore.com is an independent resource for anyone starting or growing an online store. We publish free tools, plain-English guides and honest comparisons, and we offer done-for-you builds for people who'd rather hand it off.

Why we exist

Starting an online store has never been easier, or more confusing. Every platform claims to be the best, every guru sells a course, and most new founders lose weeks to decisions that should take an afternoon. We built this site to cut that time down: clear steps, real numbers and tools that do the maths for you.

What you'll find here

  • Free tools: a platform quiz, calculators, a name generator, a description writer and a launch checklist. They all run in your browser.
  • Guides: original, practical articles reviewed and updated regularly.
  • Comparisons: qualitative, editorial assessments of popular platforms.
  • Done-for-you service: store builds, migrations and audits through our vetted expert network.

How we make money

We keep everything free through display advertising (Google AdSense), clearly labelled sponsorships, optional supporter contributions and paid store-build projects. If we ever add affiliate links, they will be disclosed on the page where they appear. Money never buys a rating, a ranking or a recommendation.

Editorial policy

  • Independence. Our ratings are our own opinion. Advertisers and sponsors can't review, edit or approve them.
  • Accuracy. We research from official documentation and hands-on use. Because platform prices and features change often, we link to official pricing pages rather than quoting numbers that go stale.
  • How we rate. Comparison stars reflect general fit for small and growing stores across ease of use, scalability and design freedom. They're editorial judgements, not lab measurements.
  • Updates. Each guide shows a "last reviewed" date. If you spot something outdated, tell us and we'll fix it.
  • No fake proof. We don't publish invented testimonials, reviews or statistics.
  • Not professional advice. Our content is general information. For legal, tax or financial decisions, consult a qualified professional in your jurisdiction.
