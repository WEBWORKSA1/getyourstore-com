Cookie Policy

Effective: November 6, 2026

What we use

TypeProviderPurpose
Essential local storageGetYourStore.comRemembers your theme, cookie choice, tool inputs, saved names, checklist/roadmap progress and form drafts. It stays on your device.

Your choices

  • Use the cookie banner: "Essential only" requests non-personalised ads; "Accept all" allows personalised ads.
  • To reset your choice, clear this site's data in your browser settings and reload the page.
  • Manage Google ad personalisation at Google Ads Settings.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some features, like saved progress, may stop working.
