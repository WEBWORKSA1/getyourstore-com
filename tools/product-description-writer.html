The formula behind descriptions that sell

Shoppers don't buy features, they buy outcomes. "Waterproof" is a feature; "rain is no problem" is the benefit. Good product copy pairs every feature with the reason it matters to the buyer, in the buyer's words.

  1. Lead with the outcome in the first sentence.
  2. Name the buyer so the right people feel spoken to.
  3. Scannable bullets for specs and features.
  4. Answer objections: sizing, materials, care, shipping, returns.
  5. One clear call to action.

Always edit the output so it's accurate for your product and adds details only you know. Unique descriptions also help SEO, while copied manufacturer text doesn't. More in Product pages that convert.
