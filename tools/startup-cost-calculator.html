Where the money actually goes

Platform fees are usually the smallest part of a startup budget. The big swings come from inventory, photography and marketing. A print-on-demand or digital store can start with almost no inventory spend; a stocked store with 30 SKUs may need thousands before launch.

Ways to start leaner

  • Pre-sell or run a waitlist to validate demand before buying stock.
  • Use a free or low-cost theme and spend the savings on product photos.
  • Add apps only when they solve a problem you actually have.
  • Start marketing with email, communities and content before paid ads.
