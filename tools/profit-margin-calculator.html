Margin vs. markup: the difference that trips people up

Markup is profit as a percentage of cost. Margin is profit as a percentage of price. A product that costs $10 and sells for $20 has a 100% markup but a 50% gross margin. Advertisers, investors and accountants talk in margin, so this calculator reports both.

The formulas

  • Fees = price × (payment % + platform %) + fixed payment fee
  • Net profit = price − product cost − shipping − fees − ad cost
  • Net margin = net profit ÷ price
  • Price for target margin = (cost + shipping + fixed fee + ad cost) ÷ (1 − payment % − platform % − target margin)

What's a good margin?

It varies by category, but after all costs including ads, many healthy small stores aim for 20–40% net margin per order. Below 15% leaves little room for returns, discounts or supplier price rises.
