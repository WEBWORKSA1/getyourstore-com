How to read your effective fee rate

Effective fee rate = (plan price + payment fees + transaction fees) ÷ revenue. It's the single number that makes plans comparable. At low volume, the monthly price dominates, so free or cheap plans win. As volume grows, percentage fees dominate, and plans with lower processing rates pull ahead.

Hidden costs to add

  • Paid apps and plugins (often charged monthly)
  • Currency conversion and cross-border card fees
  • Chargeback fees
  • Premium theme licences and renewals
  • Hosting, security and backups for self-hosted stores
