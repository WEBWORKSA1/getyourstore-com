What makes a good store name?

  • Short and sayable. If you can't say it clearly over the phone, customers can't search for it.
  • Room to grow. "Blue Mugs Only" boxes you in; a broader brand name doesn't.
  • The .com matters. Most people type .com by default. A matching domain avoids lost traffic.
  • Check trademarks. Search your country's trademark database before you print packaging. A name is only "available" if nobody holds rights to it in your market.
  • Check social handles on the platforms where your customers spend time.

The "Check" button opens the official ICANN registration lookup. If a .com is registered, try adding a short word ("shop", "co", "hq") or switch styles. Read more in our naming and domains guide.

Names are generated randomly from your keywords and common naming patterns. Generated names are not checked for trademarks; you are responsible for clearing a name before using it.
